# Map boundaries

* `india-boundary.json`, `india-states.json`: external boundary of India and state/UT boundaries as per Survey of
  India records (from github.com/AnujTiwari/India-State-and-Country-Shapefile-Updated-Jan-2020), reprojected to
  WGS84 and simplified for display.
* `rajasthan-districts-2011.json`: the 33 district boundaries of Census 2011 (from github.com/datta07/INDIAN-SHAPEFILES).
  Districts created since (Balotra, Beawar, Deeg, Didwana-Kuchaman, Khairthal-Tijara, Kotputli-Behror, Phalodi,
  Salumbar) have no published boundary in these sources; the map shows them at their headquarters.

The map draws only these files (no online map tiles), so it works offline and shows India's boundaries as published
by the Government of India.
